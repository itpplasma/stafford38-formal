# Paper and Lean specification

This maps the manuscript assertions to the formal statements. It is a
preparation snapshot; final replay and human correspondence review are pending.

## Main theorem

| Paper object | Lean specification | Checked identification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weyl algebra, `[partial_i,x_j]=delta_ij` | `WeylAlg k n`, `Matrix.J`, `FreeWeyl` | `.inl` denotes coordinates, `.inr` momenta; the commutator has the paper's sign. |
| Right-ideal certificate | `UniversalStatement` | Exactly `1=d*R+F*d*S`; no factors commute during transport. |
| Bernstein degree | `FixedSource.bernsteinDegree` | The maximum total degree in checked PBW normal form, with all generators of weight one. |
| Linear Weyl coordinate | `FixedSource.IsLinearWeylCoordinate` | A row of the inverse invertible symplectic matrix applied to the original generators. In the paper's point convention, `M=g⁻¹`, `Ninv=g`. |
| Fixed source | `FixedSource.UniversalFixedSourceStatement` | Exactly `ell^(bernsteinDegree k d)`, for positive rank; the scalar branch has degree zero. |
| Formal adjoint | Weyl `transpose` | Coordinates are fixed, momenta change sign, and products reverse. Thus the base divisor `x=0` is unchanged. |
| Canonical quotient | `canonicalRightIdeal x d N` | The right ideal generated by `d` and `x^N*d`. |
| Noncharacteristic conclusion | `CanonicalNoncharacteristicSupportAvoidance` | The transposed order-characteristic support is disjoint from the zero locus of the distinguished coordinate. |
| Gabber input | `weylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity` | Arbitrary cyclic Weyl quotient `A/I` with its order quotient filtration, over any characteristic-zero field. The paper states this exact scope, not an exported theorem for all coherent modules and all good filtrations. |

The ordinary universal statement includes rank zero, where the Weyl algebra is
the field and a nonzero scalar is invertible. A linear Weyl coordinate exists
only at positive rank. The proof map must therefore restrict the stronger
fixed-source clause to `n ≥ 1`.

The paper's positive vertical translation uses `H_f={-,f}` with
`{xi,x}=1`. Lean's `Characteristic.poissonBracket` has the opposite convention,
`{x,xi}=1`, and its `hamiltonIter` brackets the base polynomial on the left.
Thus both give the same positive vertical derivative. Involutivity and
coisotropy are invariant under this overall sign change; the two bracket
definitions must not be identified without that sign.

`Stafford38.universalFixedSourceStatement` exports the stronger conclusion
with standard axioms only, as does the ordinary root theorem.
The independent consumer `tests/CorollaryConsumer.lean` checks the literal
exponent and axiom set against the ordinary exported theorem.

Both left- and right-coefficient Ore normal forms exist. The formal outer-monic
API uses coefficients on the left. A right-coefficient display in the proof map
must be read with its stated order, not identified termwise with that API.

Coisotropy means `∀ f ∈ I, ∀ g ∈ I, {f,g} ∈ I`. The existing name
`Characteristic.IsPoisson` instead means `∀ f ∈ I, ∀ g, {f,g} ∈ I`.
That stronger predicate cannot transcribe the paper's hypothesis. The final
complex-field consumer uses ordinary fibre scaling, pointwise vanishing
of the fibre polynomial and brackets between two vanishing-ideal elements.
The zero-section ideal `(xi)` is checked to be self-involutive and not a
Poisson ideal, so this hypothesis distinction has an independent negative
control. `scripts/verify.sh` audits the geometry consumers and endpoint axioms.

The separate `cyclicity.tex` draft is not included by the main paper or
proof-map supplement. It has an incomplete equivalence argument and no Lean
counterpart; it is not an advertised result of this formal release. Its source
remains under paper ownership. Any later inclusion reopens the full theorem
coverage gate rather than inheriting the main theorem's status.

## Corollary scope

The right-Ore theorem uses the opposite of Mathlib's left Ore localization of
the opposite ring. Every denominator is proved to be a unit; nonzero fractions
have nonzero numerators. Injectivity of the numerator map is unnecessary for
this transport theorem. Its Weyl application still depends on the universal
theorem.

The evolutionary theorem assumes only that each potential coefficient commutes
with the chosen Weyl pair. It does not assume that different coefficients
commute. Its proof is independent of the geometric Stafford argument.

`LocalizedDifferentialCorollaries` proves S38 for the intrinsic algebra of
finite-order linear differential operators on an actual polynomial coefficient
localization. Its principal-open, partial Laurent, and fraction-ring consumers
state precisely the corresponding `IsLocalization` or `IsFractionRing`
hypotheses. The proof constructs the Weyl action, generates all operators, and
clears a left denominator. It does not rename a Weyl presentation as a
differential-operator ring or assume an Ore isomorphism. No theorem for arbitrary
operators on an arbitrary smooth affine variety is claimed.
