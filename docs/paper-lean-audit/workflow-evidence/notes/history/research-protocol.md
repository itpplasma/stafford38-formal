# Research protocol

## Objective

Prove or disprove Stafford 3.8 for every characteristic-zero Weyl algebra
`A_n` (with `A_2` retained as a subordinate test lane):

    ∀ d ≠ 0, ∃ F R S, 1 = d*R + F*d*S.

## Skill bindings and review tiers

Use `program-loop` plus `math-frontier` as the baseline. Activate
`evidence-gate`, `parallel-luna`, `bounded-exploration`, and
`expert-escalation` only at their stated triggers. Use `symbolic` only when
computer algebra feeds Fortran and `referee` only for manuscript review.

For an ordinary meaningful lemma, computation, or route check, a cheap
ephemeral Luna micro-review may be used. It is a working-context sanity check,
not a promotion gate and does not require a claim record or commit. Routine
PDF reading, scratch calculations, failed attempts, and `NO_PROGRESS` likewise
remain ephemeral unless a blocker, decision, or reusable result must survive
restart.

## Completion conditions

The project is complete only after committing and reviewing one of:

1. a complete independently checkable universal proof;
2. a complete independently checkable counterexample; or
3. a hard-stop report with no executable open leaf and an exact external
   theorem, computation, or human decision still required.

## One cycle

The detailed two-speed loop is in `notes/history/operating-loop.md`. In summary:

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `PLAN.md`, and `notes/history/task-pool.yaml`,
   and the active verifier/evidence paths. Load the roadmap, literature,
   ledgers, templates, and review protocols only when named by the task or at
   integration.
2. Select exactly one `OPEN` active task and make at most one substantive
   attack on it.
3. Execute its verifier and record `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, or `NO_PROGRESS`
   with evidence and scope.
4. Integrate after four verified deltas, three `NO_PROGRESS` cycles, closure
   or refutation, a conceptual block, or an interface change.
5. At a genuine conceptual fork, dispatch GPT-Sol lanes only on distinct
   frozen mechanisms. Independently verify every returned suggestion; do not
   count repeated questions or additional special cases as parallel progress.
6. Use optional micro-review for ordinary meaningful work; use focused review
   and the full evidence gate only for durable mathematical deltas,
   promotions, and significant certificates. Persist only what must survive
   restart, and never change the terminal mission from a bounded result.

## Valid frontier deltas

- a proved noncircular implication from established hypotheses;
- an explicit counterexample that refutes a route;
- Stafford 3.8 for a new, precisely delimited class of `d`;
- an executed exact computation with input, output, verification, and scope;
- a verified literature theorem with every hypothesis checked;
- a formal correction that invalidates a route currently used by the project.

## Invalid pseudo-progress

- restating `M = xA + zA`;
- unrestricted `q` selection;
- a sufficient condition that already contains `x ∈ zA`;
- an unexecuted script or bounded ansatz presented as a theorem;
- genericity without a finite proved avoidance theorem;
- left/right transport without an explicit opposite-ring proof;
- an equality in `A` obtained only in `A/dA`;
- formalizing a documented boundary without closing an active leaf.

Negative computations are bounded claims about their declared search pool.
Positive certificates must be re-expanded and checked in the original
noncommutative presentation.
