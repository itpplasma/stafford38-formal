# Workflow evidence

The paper's AI appendix is supported by the [history reconstruction](workflow-history.txt),
[prompt reconstruction](workflow-prompts.txt), and [manifest](manifest.json).
The reports distinguish recorded sessions, prescribed model roles, surviving
prompt excerpts, and later editorial work. They do not attribute a discovery
to one model merely because its name occurs in a protocol.

The historical protocols under `notes/history/` and the historical skills give
the exact surviving text behind the representative prompt. The skills, with
their CC0 notice, and the model-use inventory, with its stated scan scope, are
in release
[v1.3.1](https://github.com/itpplasma/stafford38-formal/tree/v1.3.1/docs/paper-lean-audit/workflow-evidence/archive).
These sources are provenance; they are not additional
premises or mathematical verification of the Lean theorem. Dates in these
evidence files identify source revisions; the paper's narrative remains general.

The research source files retain their original project license. The manifest
records the source commit and each copied file hash. Raw assistant sessions
are not required to run the formal verifier.
