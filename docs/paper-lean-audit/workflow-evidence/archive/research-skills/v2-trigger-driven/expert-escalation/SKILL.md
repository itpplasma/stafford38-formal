# Expert Escalation

Use after bounded exploration or two materially distinct failed attempts when
specialist knowledge is genuinely required. Do not use for routine coding,
formatting, simple debugging, or work answerable from project state.

## Consultation packet

Include the exact mission and active task, verified facts with evidence paths,
current evidence, failed and blacklisted routes, constraints, tools, budget,
one bounded question, and the falsification test.

Ask for at most three candidate mechanisms. Each must state its intermediate
claim, assumptions, noncircularity, cheapest falsification test, first
bottleneck, artifact, and next executable action.

## Status and verification

Every response is `PROPOSED`. Advice is not evidence, a task pass, or a
milestone promotion. The controller must independently test or verify it,
preserve rejected alternatives when useful, and use `evidence-gate` and the
required `parallel-luna` tier before promotion.

If no verifier-bearing route remains, return to `program-loop` with a reviewed
hard stop or a new exploration packet. Do not escalate repeatedly without new
evidence or a changed question. Experts are report-only; they do not edit
authoritative state.
