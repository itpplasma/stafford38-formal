The Global Stafford result~\cite{GlobalStaffordPalomar2026}
extends the same-divisor identity to every smooth
integral finitely generated algebra $A$ over a characteristic-zero field $k$:
for every nonzero finite-order $k$-linear differential operator $d\in D_k(A)$,
there exist $F,R,S\in D_k(A)$ with $1=dR+FdS$, where multiplication is
composition. Taking $A=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ recovers the Weyl-algebra identity
proved here. The extension allows arbitrary smooth integral affine varieties;
it asserts existence of $F$, without the fixed-source refinement
$F=\ell^{\deg_B d}$ proved here.
