## Frozen gaps and checked repairs

1. **Order-symbol monicity.** Frozen `human_readable_main.tex` lines 691–717 gives the normal form `d̃ = p^N + Σ c_j p^j` (left coefficients), but the proof at lines 710–717 reasons as if the summands were `p^j a_j`. That inference does not establish that no other order-`N` term contributes to `ξ₁^N`. The live proof at lines 713–725 expands each `c_j` in PBW monomials: an order-`N` term in `c_j p^j` has transverse derivative order `N−j` and zero coordinate degree by `deg_B c_j ≤ N−j`; since `j<N`, it contains a transverse derivative and cannot contribute to `ξ₁^N`. This proves the stated monicity and `P(1,0,…,0)=1` under the displayed hypotheses.

2. **Page definitions and exact successor bridge.** In the frozen active proof, lines 1060–1067 invoke “the printed formulas,” while the only formulas are in the percent-commented historical block. Thus the active proof did not define its pages or supply the exact kernel/cokernel bridge needed for the length telescope. The live proof at lines 1072–1132 defines `A_r^p`, `B_r^p`, and `d_r`; verifies well-definedness and the `T`-action; identifies `A_{r+1}` and `B_{r+1}` with kernel and cokernel (including the target boundary-denominator enlargement); then proves the exact five-term sequence. It chooses one preimage using `Q=Qx₁`, uses exhaustiveness to kill each class on a later page, localizes, and telescopes finite lengths. The matching COMP-06 supplement block is explicit as well. The formulas and sequence agree with the checked page constructions; the single-preimage argument supplies the required exhaustion without an iterated-preimage assumption.
